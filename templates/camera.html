Multimodal Architecture & Viva Notes

How vision indicators feed into engagement scoring

1. Multimodal Sensing Pipeline

In a production smart classroom, physical sensors capture multiple data modalities:

  • Visual Modality: Optical feed calculates head pose, gaze focus, and physical movement deltas between frames.
  • Acoustic Modality: Ambient microphone measures audio decibel levels and conversational frequency to gauge peer discussions.
  • Temporal Modality: Tracks continuous inactive lecture minutes to trigger timely teaching breaks.

2. Privacy-by-Design Compliance

Unlike invasive facial recognition that matches student faces against biometric databases, this system only counts general presence and head movement vectors. No biometric embeddings, facial coordinates, or personal images are permanently stored in SQLite.

3. Graceful Fallback Design

Even if the classroom computer does not have a webcam or the sensor fails, the instructor can manually select classroom indicators on the Engagement Analytics page without disrupting attendance tracking.
